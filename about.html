All About Roblox

Roblox is a popular global online platform and game creation system, launched in 2006, where users play and create games for others to enjoy.The platform uses "Robux," a virtual currency that can be purchased to buy in-game items, avatars, and accessories. While popular with children it is used by all ages. It is used as a way to express creativity. People build games, join other peoples games, and have fun.Some popular games are steal a brainrot, flee the facility, and bloxburg.
