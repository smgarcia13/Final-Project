All About Roblox

Roblox is a popular global online platform and game creation system, launched in 2006, where users play and create games fro others to enjoy.The platform uses "Robux," a virtual currency that can be purchased to buy in-game items, avatars, and accessories. While popular with children it is used by all ages.
